## Battle strategies

Each strategy uses 5 Attacks, 3 Shields, and 2 Shieldbreaker bombs. Shieldbreaker deals double normal damage against Shield and zero otherwise. Normal shield blocks keep Counter and next-turn Riposte. Replays and history show only your moves, score changes, and damage dealt; opponent strategies stay private. Existing battle results remain available.
