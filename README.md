# Pokebar releases

Download [Pokebar 2.5.91](https://github.com/actioneerhimi/poketokenbar-releases/releases/tag/v2.5.91).

Claude official-limit checks now refresh every five minutes while local token counting keeps its normal schedule. Retry respects a 429 cooldown, shows the remaining wait, and keeps that cooldown after restarting. Repeated clicks no longer send extra requests or extend the wait to an hour.

Includes the redesigned Home, battle planning, and Settings from 2.5.90. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel on macOS 14 or later. The app and installer are signed and notarized; minimum version, rewards, prices, saves, and battle policies are unchanged.
