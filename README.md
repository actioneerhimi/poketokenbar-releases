# Pokebar releases

Download [Pokebar 2.5.89](https://github.com/actioneerhimi/poketokenbar-releases/releases/tag/v2.5.89).

Turn on Auto strategy in Battles → Battle strategy to change your move order daily at UTC reset and after every defeat. Earn Legendary Eggs at 100, 200, 300… lifetime wins, and Rare Eggs at 50, 150, 250… wins. Earned eggs arrive in your Bag once per milestone.

Rare Candy can now finish an incubating egg and saves unused XP for the hatched Pokémon and subsequent evolution, including after restart or an interrupted hatch. Whole surplus candies stay in the Bag. Approved goodwill gifts add candy inventory without deducting XP and arrive once after updating and syncing. Existing refunds and free graduation eggs remain supported.

Export a problem report from Settings or a Pokémon verification notice. Preview the report, save it as a text file, and attach it to Slack yourself. Pokebar does not send reports automatically.

New Legendary Eggs cost 5B tokens after updating to 2.5.88 and guarantee a Legendary or Mythical Pokémon. Purchases made before updating keep their original 10B accounting. Other prices and rewards are unchanged. Mint, Rare Egg and evolution boosters remain removed from the shop.

Report sits immediately left of Retry and includes the current verification issue. Leaderboard entries with no token activity in the last seven days are hidden and return automatically with new activity.

Online Pokémon verification, leaderboards and regular battles keep existing progress. Live Battles beta remains removed.

Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14 or later. The app and installer are signed and notarized. Updates use the existing signed update feed.

## Usage history and pet reactions

History on Home's usage card shows daily tokens for the last 7, 30, or 90 UTC days across every tool, with cost, daily average, busiest day, and per-tool and per-model shares. Today's bar matches Home; older days are read from the logs on your Mac.

The floating pet reacts to your coding agents: it works along while they run, gets in the zone after 45 unbroken minutes, sweats near a limit, naps when they're idle, and celebrates when a long session wraps. Turn this off in Settings → Floating pet → React to your agents.

## Battle strategies

Each strategy uses 5 Attacks, 3 Shields, and 2 Shieldbreaker bombs. Shieldbreaker deals double normal damage against Shield and zero otherwise. Normal shield blocks keep Counter and next-turn Riposte. Replays and history show only your moves, score changes, and damage dealt; opponent strategies stay private. Existing battle results remain available.

## New in 2.5.88

Faster Claude Code refreshes, provider-colored daily charts with per-day details, and collectable alternate Pokemon appearances. Existing saves, collections, 5B/legacy 10B purchases, receipts and candy accounting are preserved. Existing Pokemon are not rerolled.

## New in 2.5.89

Pokémon expeditions start and collect automatically, with no Explore screen. Forest, Cave and Coast take 2, 4 and 6 hours and award Rare Candy. Existing Oran Berries convert one-for-one for eligible legacy accounts, up to 100. Other items, saves, collections and receipts are preserved.

New reward candies verify without another refresh, and battle startup no longer waits for unrelated history loading. Automatic battles remain disabled.
